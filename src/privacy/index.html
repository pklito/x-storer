XBookmark

Privacy Policy & Terms of Use — Last updated: March 10, 2026

What is XBookmark?

XBookmark is a free, open-source browser extension that saves your X (Twitter) bookmarks locally on your device. It allows you to organize bookmarks with tags and export them in Markdown or JSON format.

Privacy Policy

XBookmark collects zero data. No personal information, no usage analytics, no tracking, no telemetry. Nothing leaves your browser.

Data Storage

All bookmark data is stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB. No external servers, databases, or cloud services are used. Your data stays on your machine.

Network Requests

XBookmark stores all data locally. The dashboard displays cached tweet images from Twitter's CDN (pbs.twimg.com). No data is sent to any API, analytics service, or remote server.

Permissions

  • activeTab — Read the current tab's content when on x.com or twitter.com to detect bookmarked tweets
  • host_permissions (x.com, twitter.com) — Required for the content script to run on X/Twitter pages

Third-Party Services

XBookmark uses no third-party services, SDKs, analytics, or tracking of any kind.

Data Deletion

Uninstalling the extension removes all stored data. You can also delete individual bookmarks from the dashboard at any time.
